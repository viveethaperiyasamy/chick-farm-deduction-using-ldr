    **Automatic Chick Farm Light Control System Using LDR Sensor**

**Aim**  
            To design and implement an automatic lighting system for a poultry farm using an LDR sensor, so that the light automatically turns ON when the surrounding light level is low, providing warmth and suitable conditions for chicks to grow.

**Objective**  
            •To detect the surrounding light intensity using an LDR sensor.  
            •To automatically switch ON the bulb when sufficient light is not available.  
            •To provide continuous warmth and a suitable environment for chicks.   
            •To reduce the need for manual switching of the bulb.

**Components Required**  
            1\. LDR (Light Dependent Resistor)  
            2\. LED/Bulb  
            3\. Transistor  
            4\. Resistors  
            5\. Breadboard  
            6\. Connecting wires  
            7\. Battery/DC power supply  
            8\. Switch  
            9\. Poultry farm/chick model setup

**Working Principle**  
            •The system works based on the light-sensitive property of the LDR.  
            •When there is sufficient surrounding light, the LDR senses the light and the bulb remains OFF.  
            •When the surrounding light becomes low or dark, the LDR detects the reduction in light intensity.      
            •The sensor circuit then activates the switching device.  
            •The bulb automatically turns ON.  
            •The bulb provides the required warmth/light for the chicks and helps maintain a suitable environment for their growth.

**Procedure**  
         1\. Connect the LDR sensor to the breadboard circuit.  
         2\. Connect the required resistors and transistor as the switching circuit.  
         3\. Connect the bulb/LED to the output of the switching circuit.  
         4\. Connect the battery/DC supply to the circuit.  
         5\. Place the LDR where it can sense the surrounding light.  
         6\. Expose the LDR to normal light and observe the bulb.  
         7\. Cover the LDR or reduce the surrounding light.  
         8\. Observe that the bulb automatically turns ON when the light level becomes low.

**Result**  
The LDR-based automatic poultry lighting system was successfully designed  and implemented. The bulb automatically turns ON when the surrounding light is insufficient, providing continuous light and warmth for the chicks.

**Applications**  
          •Poultry farms  
          •Automatic night lighting  
          •Animal shelters  
          •Small-scale farming automation

**Advantages**  
        •Automatic operation  
         •Reduces manual monitoring  
         •Simple and low-cost circuit  
         •Saves energy by switching the light according to light conditions  
         •Helps maintain a suitable environment for chicks

**Conclusion**  
         The project demonstrates how an LDR sensor can be used to automate lighting in a poultry farm. The system detects low light conditions and automatically switches ON the bulb, reducing manual operation and helping provide a suitable environment for chicks.
